What happens to your clip

Most removers paint over the watermark with a guess. DYNA ai works out exactly what the watermark did to each pixel and undoes it.

1

Find it

Edges that stay put while everything else moves are the watermark. DYNA ai scans the whole clip to find them, or you paint them yourself.

2

Measure it

It learns how see-through the watermark is at every pixel, separately for brightness and for colour, from every frame of the clip.

3

Subtract it

In every frame it re-checks how strong the watermark is, then removes exactly that much. What's left is the original picture.

4

Finish the edges

The watermark's thin outline is rebuilt from its neighbours. On smooth backgrounds, leftover compression marks are evened out, but real edges stay sharp.
